P4 · AI assurance case for a lending model

Is the P1 model acceptable for its stated lending use — and where is the evidence still missing?

A claim–argument–evidence assurance case for the P1 credit model in a defined lending use. It links every requirement to a test and to evidence from P1–P3 where available, and records missing evidence openly as assurance gaps.
